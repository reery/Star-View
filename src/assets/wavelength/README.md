# Wavelength vision panoramas

All-sky survey maps shown behind the map when Wavelength vision shows a band other than visible light. Each loads only the first time its band is selected, so none adds to startup. Generated by `scripts/author-wavelength-panoramas.py` from NASA SkyView resamplings (https://skyview.gsfc.nasa.gov) on 2026-11-16.

## Delivery

AVIF, 2048 × 1024 pixels, Galactic plate carrée laid out like `../milky-way.avif`: the Galactic center in the middle, north up, longitude increasing to the left. The red channel holds the band's longer-wavelength survey, the green channel its shorter one; blue is empty. The app tints the two with the band's false colors. Values are a logarithmic stretch from the faint background (15th percentile) to the brightest regions (99.8th percentile). ROSAT scan gaps and IRAS gaps are filled from the surrounding sky; Fermi and ROSAT maps are smoothed below their resolution to remove photon noise.

There is no ultraviolet panorama (GALEX does not cover the whole sky) and no near-infrared one (SkyView offers no all-sky near-infrared map; COBE DIRBE returns only its 100 µm band).
