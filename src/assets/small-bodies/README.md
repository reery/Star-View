# Small-body imagery

All assets are observational. No generated terrain, invented surface color or stretched partial photographs. Main photographs are capped at 836 px on the longest side, without upscaling; moon-only crops are capped at 418 × 344 px. Low-resolution telescope detections remain unresolved.

## Pluto globe

`pluto-map.jpg` (2048 × 1024): NASA/VTAD Pluto model texture, extracted from `Pluto_1_2374.glb`. Approximate visible color from New Horizons observations; non-encounter hemisphere is much less resolved. The unobserved southern cap is masked to plain gray in the renderer rather than presenting the model’s interpolation as measured terrain. Lighting and orientation are illustrative. Model: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-3d-model/ . NASA image use: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ .

## Pluto moons

- `charon-photo.jpg`: 344 × 344; NASA / JHUAPL / SwRI / Alex Parker. New Horizons MVIC, July 14, 2015; refined natural-color processing released in 2018. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/true-colors-of-charon/ . Public domain.
- `styx-photo.jpg`, `nix-photo.jpg`, `kerberos-photo.jpg`, `hydra-photo.jpg`: individual 236 × 236 crops of NASA / JHUAPL / SwRI’s New Horizons family portrait (2015). Monochrome, original detector resolution retained; no upscaling or added color. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/charon-small-moons-of-pluto/ . Public domain.
- `*-moons-photo.jpg`: copies of the corresponding credited system photographs above, reduced to at most 344 px on the longest side without upscaling. Both primary and companion remain in view; no inferred moon surfaces. The Varda portrait does not resolve Ilmarë, so it is not reused as an image of Ilmarë.
