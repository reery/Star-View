# Component research for the 37 source records

Reviewed 2026-10-06. Scope: companions of the original 37 flagged records (36 systems), within 25 pc. This is a literature review; these proposals have not yet been applied to the catalogs. Existing catalog/UI work remains as it was.

The review identifies **33 supported additional objects**: 29 star, 3 white dwarf, 1 brown dwarf. Two already cataloged objects need association with their systems. **13 records retain an unresolved companion or naming question**; this does not mean they were left unresearched.

New-object counts exclude the provisional GJ 595 B interpretation and all held companion slots. GJ 400 has a secure resolved identity but needs its B/C naming scope reconciled before card authoring. Xi UMa C is counted once even though it appears in both its own source record and the central hierarchy review.

## Authoring notes

- Split combined component records before assigning individual specs. Withhold blended photometry and unsupported individual physical fields.
- Use each resolved wide companion's own astrometry; only unresolved close components may share an explicitly approximate source position.
- Match exact identifiers rather than display aliases. Ross 775 has a conflicting published table label; LTT 1445, Xi UMa, GJ 4, GJ 507 and GJ 400 have incompatible component-letter conventions between sources.
- Several companions have empirical or model-derived masses rather than dynamical masses. Methods and asymmetric uncertainties must remain visible in provenance.
- The JSON review records exact Gaia/Karmn identities, proposed stable IDs, held components and individual measurement candidates. The frozen CARMENES extraction retains original rows and their bibliography.
