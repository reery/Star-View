# Compact nearby systems: reviewed 18 October 2026

The nine components of Gliese 65, Ross 614, Wolf 424 and GJ 1245 now use one
Sun-relative Galactic velocity vector per confirmed system. This preserves their
relative positions during the motion display. Proxima is excluded. No proximity
threshold or automatic copying to other systems is introduced.

| System | Shared radial velocity (km/s) | Motion basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Luyten 726-8 / Gliese 65 AB | +39.04 ± 0.20 | Kervella 2016 fitted barycentric RV and published system proper motion/parallax |
| V577 Mon / Ross 614 AB | +16.18 | Benedict 2016 fitted gamma and system proper motion/parallax; no formal gamma error supplied |
| FL Vir / Wolf 424 AB | +1 ± 4 | Existing unresolved Terrien 2015 RV; mass-weighted mean of retained component velocity snapshots |
| GJ 1245 ABC | +5.43 ± 0.10 | Well-separated B component astrometry/RV used as an approximate ABC bulk vector |

The last two are proxies, not fitted systemic solutions. Quoted RV measurement
errors do not quantify unmodeled orbital motion or astrometric systematics.
Original component proper motions, positions, velocities, photometry,
uncertainties and provenance are retained in the frozen review and effective
component source records. Raw component astrometry therefore need not reproduce
the separately adopted bulk vector. A/B/C identities are unchanged.

Resolved dynamical masses and visual photometry now reach both nearby catalogs:
Ross 614 A/B use 0.223/0.109 solar masses (Benedict 2016); Wolf 424 A/B use
0.1379/0.1258 (Chevalier 2023). GJ 1245 A/C use revised dynamical masses
0.120/0.081 and resolved HST/STIS atmospheric estimates (Dieterich 2021).
Resolved GJ 1245 B estimates come from Cifuentes 2025 Table A.1, exact Gaia ID
2079073928612821760; its source component label C maps to the app's wide B.
GJ 1245 luminosities/radii/temperatures retain their model or calibration scope.

Radius and luminosity remain unavailable for Ross 614 and Wolf 424. The newer
Gaia-based photometric estimates have close-pair blending/orbital caveats;
Ross 614 B is explicitly flagged for a Gaia/WDS brightness mismatch. An
unresolved temperature is not assigned to both components. Unsupported ages and
abundances remain unavailable.

The machine-readable review contains source URLs, individual uncertainties,
rejected alternatives, the exact downloaded Cifuentes rows and complete before
snapshots: [compact-system-review.json](../catalog-work/shared-enrichment/compact-system-review.json).
